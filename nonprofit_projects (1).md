## Project 1: Chipata Girls Education Program

### Project Description

The Chipata Girls Education Program supports young women in Chipata, Zambia, by providing comprehensive financial assistance to pursue secondary and university-level education. Recognizing that girls in this region face significant systemic barriers to education — including financial hardship, cultural pressures, and lack of safe housing — this program removes those obstacles by covering the full cost of attendance.

Currently, the program supports **6 girls** in secondary and university education. Over the next three years, the goal is to expand the program to support **30 students**, transforming the program into a regional model for girls' education in Zambia.

Support is **recurring, semester-based**, and covers housing, tuition fees, academic materials, and living costs.

### Current Impact
- 6 girls currently enrolled and supported
- Coverage: housing, education fees, and living costs
- Duration: ongoing, per-semester support

## Project 4: Atoot & The WE United Project — Nepal

### Project Description

**The WE United Project** is a women-led initiative founded in Kathmandu, Nepal, built on the belief that female participation in sport drives broader empowerment and equality for women and girls. Through physical activity, the project builds assets, provides access to resources, and develops agency among its participants.

**Atoot** operates out of Kapilvasu, Nepal, with a mission to open educational and sporting opportunities to girls in the region. Atoot uses Sports for Development (S4D) as a catalyst for empowerment and holistic youth development.

Together, these two organizations represent a cross-regional partnership connecting community-level girls' sport in rural Nepal with a growing national movement in Kathmandu.

## Project 5: FSMART+ Africa 2030 — Niger

### Project Description

The Football Smart Africa 2030 Association (F.SMART+) is an apolitical, non-discriminatory organization based in Agadez, Niger, committed to the educational, sporting, and socio-cultural emancipation of young girls and boys in the Agadez region. Using football as the catalyst, F.SMART+ works toward the achievement of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Since 2020, F.SMART+ has implemented the **"Second Chance Program"** on an annual basis — acquiring and distributing free school bags, notebooks, pens, pencils, slates, books, geometric sets, and more to orphaned children and primary school students in the Agadez area.

## Project 6: H.I.P. — Head Impact Prevention — USA

### Project Description

H.I.P. (Head Impact Prevention) Football was founded by Tim Johnson, a former NFL player known for his memorable blocked punt touchdown in Super Bowl 37. Tim played for the Baltimore Ravens, Chicago Bears, and Oakland Raiders (NFL), the Calgary Stampeders (CFL), and Rhein Fire (NFL Europe) before retiring and channeling his expertise into athlete safety.

H.I.P. Football uses a **non-contact coaching model** — teaching key football skills and movement in a two-hand touch format. This prevents brain impact injuries while preparing athletes to excel when they transition to full-contact teams and leagues.

**H.I.P. MD** has developed a next-generation American Football Helmet, combining biomimicking innovations with planned integration of sensor technologies to monitor force impact, pulse, and electrical brain activity — protecting brain health and supporting the longevity of athletes on the field.

H.I.P. operates out of Ohio and Oregon, USA.
